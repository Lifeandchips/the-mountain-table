Our Story

Dining Inspired by the Himalayas

The Mountain Table is a fictional premium dining destination imagined for Gangtok, where the character of the Himalayas meets a contemporary approach to hospitality. Our menu brings together familiar mountain flavours, modern Indian cooking, and carefully selected international favourites.

The idea began with a simple belief: good food should feel connected to its surroundings. Seasonal produce, warming spices, delicate herbs and comforting Himalayan recipes form the foundation of a menu designed for slow lunches, intimate dinners and memorable evenings with family and friends.
